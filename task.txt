Submit Terraform-based automation that brings up the complete lab with one documented
command after credentials and variables are supplied. No manual server, console, or Wazuh
setup. Any one cloud is acceptable.

Build checklist
● Application: Deploy OWASP Juice Shop on Kubernetes, preferably K3s on a VM.
Docker on a VM is also accepted.
● Wazuh: Deploy the server, indexer, and dashboard on a separate VM. Automate
agent/collector installation, enrollment, and application/ingress log collection. Persist
Wazuh data.
● VPN: Provide working private access to Wazuh and administration, using OpenVPN,
WireGuard, or equivalent private-network access.
● WAF: Put Juice Shop behind a WAF. Demonstrate one allowed request and one
deterministic blocked request. Prevent direct-origin bypass; restrict the lab to VPN users
or an evaluator IP allowlist.
● Cloud security: Restrict firewall rules and identities. Protect secrets and Terraform
state; commit neither credentials nor state. Document TLS handling.
● Code: Write a verifier that checks readiness, sends a request with a new unique marker,
and finds that marker in Wazuh through its Indexer API. Configure a test rule or archive
indexing as needed. Use timeouts and nonzero failure exits. Deployment succeeds only
after readiness and log delivery pass; reruns must preserve data.
● CI/CD: Validate Terraform, test the verifier, and scan candidate code/IaC and secrets.
Gate the deployment job on successful checks. Use protected credentials and a
controlled trigger. Juice Shop vulnerability scanning is optional; do not disable other
checks for its intentional vulnerabilities.

Keep spending low
● Use two small VMs and free tools. Managed Kubernetes, high availability, and paid
VPN/WAF subscriptions are optional. Suggested app VM: 2 vCPU / 4 GB RAM. Wazuh's
starting recommendation: 4 vCPU / 8 GiB RAM / 50 GB disk.
● Use eligible trial credits: Google Cloud — $300 or AWS — up to $200 ($100 initially, up
to $100 earned). Check account eligibility and expiry. Estimate the short lab run's total
cost, including disks, public IPs, and networking; tear down assessment resources after
testing.

Tool links — choose what fits
● Core: Terraform, K3s, Juice Shop, Wazuh, Wazuh Docker deployment.
● Open-source VPN: OpenVPN Community or WireGuard.
● Open-source WAF: ModSecurity + OWASP CRS Docker or Coraza + Caddy. No paid
WAF or public domain is needed for a private lab.
● Free hosted alternative: Cloudflare WAF includes a free managed ruleset and 5
custom rules for a domain you control. Zero Trust Free supports up to 50 users. Tunnel
with private-network access requires an enrolled Cloudflare One/WARP client; a public
Tunnel alone is not a VPN.
● Open-source CI checks: Trivy and Gitleaks. Use any CI platform.
